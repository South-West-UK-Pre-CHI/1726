Directional signs be posted inside an’ outside the buildin’ fer every room, the heads, an’ the lift. 
The grand gatherin’ be held in the Bill Brown Design Suite (room F.101 a/b/c), tucked away in the basement o’ the Queen’s Buildin’.
A map o’ the place can be found on [MazeMap](https://link.mazemap.com/ya4oy3EV), if ye fancy a peek.

The easiest way to board the venue be through the door on Woodland Road, ye’ll also find the ramp there fer smooth sailin’.

There be automatic doors at the entrance, but they need a pirate's UCard to open. Kind crewmates will be standin’ by to let ye through.

Once ye step inside, the main conference quarters be off to yer starboard side (that’s the right, ye landlubber).

If ye be wantin’ a full guided tour with pictures, cast yer eyes upon the [Access an' Inclusion Plan](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1EFZFd0djQXFNtJB2qIjqMsXmU95Qylhe/edit?usp=sharing&ouid=102504969469176201445&rtpof=true&sd=true).
